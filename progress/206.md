# #206 Hosted alert per-channel retry

- Landed on main in 0e33c48: each alert occurrence keeps `deliveryChannels` (`accepted`/`failed`/`unconfigured`/`gave_up`) and `deliveryRetry` (`attempts`, `next_at`). The sweep resends only `failed` channels once due (backoff 1, 2, 4 … min, capped at 30 min). After 10 attempts the channel is marked `gave_up`, and exactly one `alert.delivery_gave_up` audit row is written. Accepted channels are never resent for that occurrence. A new occurrence delivers to every configured channel. The aggregate `delivery` stays for compatibility. A channel configured after the alert fired is not back-filled. README lifecycle text updated.
